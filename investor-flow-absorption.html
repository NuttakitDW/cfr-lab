Investor-Flow Absorption

Original hypothesis — untested Back to index
Hypothesis Taiwan · TWSE · 16 Aug 2026

Everybody watches the foreign flow. Nobody reads who was willing to sell into it.

Market Taiwan · TWSE Raw material daily per-security investor-category trading Type cross-sectional equity Stage hypothesis, unbacktested

Taiwan publishes something almost no other market does: a daily, per-security breakdown of who traded, split by investor category — foreign investors, dealer proprietary desks, dealer hedge desks, and domestic trusts. Foreign institutional buying is the most-watched number in the region and therefore worthless as an edge. The counterparty is not watched at all.

The claim: a large slice of foreign flow is mandated rather than informed, so the gap between local discretionary accumulation and the foreign bid carries information that neither flow carries alone — and its sign runs opposite to the conventional wisdom about who the smart money is. The subtraction only means anything once both sides are put on a common scale, and the scaling choice is where the idea most easily collapses back into a crowded factor.

The sharpest falsification test is the split dealer book: the proprietary desks trade on a view, the hedge desks trade mechanically. If the two point the same way, the framing is wrong. This is written up as a hypothesis, not a result — there is no backtest behind it and no source paper to link.

Provenance

No source paper — this is an original hypothesis.

The framing came out of the debris of forty machine-generated hypotheses that were all killed or unbuildable: a pattern across the failures (every dead idea collapsed a rich per-record field into one summary number) plus a leftover field listing from an unrelated dead end that happened to contain the investor-category breakdown. The models’ contribution was negative information — a map of what is already dead. That is real value; it is not authorship.

Untested idea. Nothing here has been backtested, and it depends entirely on a disclosure regime unique to Taiwan — it cannot be lifted to a venue that does not publish the same breakdown. Treat it as a research direction, not a claim.

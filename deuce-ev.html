Setup

Deuce-to-seven lowball: the ace is always high, and straights and flushes count against you — so 7-5-4-3-2 is the nut and 7-6-5-4-3 is a straight. Give a seat five cards or leave it face down for a random hand, pick how many draws are still to come, and run it. Both seats then draw out the hand under the same policy, so the equity is the real thing rather than a showdown between frozen hands.
